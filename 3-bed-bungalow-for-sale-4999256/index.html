About the property

Description property

Paul Lafferty Ltd Auctioneers are delighted to present to the market this charming c.1950s three-bedroom bungalow set on an elevated site of approximately 0.6 acres at Sonvolaun, Kilmovee, Co. Mayo.

While requiring refurbishment and modernisation, the property retains a number of attractive original features, including internal doors, architraves and feature fireplaces, offering excellent potential to create a beautiful family or holiday home.

Accommodation

Externally, the property enjoys generous garden areas to the front and rear, along with a number of outbuildings including an old hay shed and garage at Roadside. Mature trees along the rear boundary provide excellent privacy and a tranquil rural setting.

An additional parcel of approximately 5 acres of grazing land, located c.400 metres from the residence, is available as an optional purchase.

This property may qualify for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant and Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland (SEAI) retrofit grants, subject to certain conditions
